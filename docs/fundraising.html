Data notes
  • Raised Q3 is total receipts (Line 16) on the pre-primary report plus the October Quarterly, so it covers July 1 through September 30 for every candidate. Receipts include candidate loans and transfers, not only contributions.
  • Spent Q3 is total disbursements (Line 22) for the same reports. Burn rate is spent divided by raised; over 100% means the campaign spent down its bank.
  • Cash on hand is Line 27 as of September 30. Raised cycle and Spent cycle are cycle-to-date totals (Lines 16 and 22, column B).
  • Small-dollar share is unitemized individual contributions (Line 11(a)(ii), donors giving $200 or less this cycle) divided by total receipts. Party totals pool every candidate's dollars rather than averaging percentages.
  • Candidate loans are loans made by the candidate (Line 13(a)) and debt is debts owed by the committee (Line 10), where the research sheet recorded them. The Frontier and Horizon tabs didn't track these, so they show as zero there.
  • Kevin Kiley (CA-06) filed as an independent and is counted with Republicans. Bill Hill (AK-AL), an independent, is counted on the Democratic side, matching the IE tracker. Other third-party candidates appear in each race's detail but aren't in the party totals.
  • Outside spending in each race's detail comes from the House IE Tracker (independent expenditures plus party coordinated spending, filings through ).
  • DCCC program comes from the DCCC's 2026 Frontline and Red to Blue pages as of Oct 16, 2026 (26 Frontline members, 46 Red to Blue candidates). Every other race is marked Districts in Play.
  • NRCC program comes from the NRCC's Patriots and MAGA Majority sheets (17 Patriots, 27 MAGA Majority). James Gallagher (CA-01, Patriots) isn't in the research sheet. Republicans on neither list show a dash.
  • Incumbents are marked (i). Source: FEC Master Sheet, 2026 Q3, research tabs.
